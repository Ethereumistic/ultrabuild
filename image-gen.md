# Ultrabuild Inventory - Image Generation Prompts (Optimized for 1:1 & Clean Background)

**Prompt Engineering Notes:**
* **Clean Focus:** Terms like "solo," "in front of warehouse," and "empty exterior concrete surface" force the AI to keep the background completely distraction-free.
* **Negative Prompting:** If the generator you are using (like Nano Banana) has a "Negative Prompt" field, always copy and paste this into it: `other vehicles, people, marketing flags, banners, text, logos, clutter, messy background, cropped`
* **Aspect Ratio:** The prompts explicitly state "1:1 aspect ratio, perfectly centered."
